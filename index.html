Station 1: Unknown Site Fluid Lab

Drag weights onto the hydrometer pan & align the red sightline to read $h$. On the right, drag the reference block onto the hook & pull the scale down to submerge it.

Procedure Steps

  1. Stack different brass weights on the hydrometer pan and align the red sightline with the waterline to read $h$.
  2. Compute $\rho_f = \dfrac{m_h}{A h}$ across 3 different mass trials and average the results.
  3. Hook the reference block to read $W_{\text{air}}$, lower it fully underwater for $W_{\text{sub}}$, and verify $\rho_f = \dfrac{W_{\text{air}} - W_{\text{sub}}}{V_r g}$.
